Meld

Finds phone videos of a concert online, lines them up by their sound and combines them into one video with
the best of the sound.

FOR PERSONAL USE ONLY
Meld downloads videos from YouTube. Doing so may go against YouTube's terms of service, and concert
footage is usually protected by copyright. Use it for your own viewing and do not publish or share the videos
it makes.

Software included
- FFmpeg, for reading and writing video and sound (a build licensed under the GNU GPL; source code is
  available from https://ffmpeg.org).
- yt-dlp, for downloading (public domain, Unlicense).
- Python, NumPy and SciPy (PSF / BSD licenses).

Meld keeps a few small files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Meld (an updated copy of the downloader and your
last choices). The videos it makes are saved in the folder you choose, by default Documents\Meld.
